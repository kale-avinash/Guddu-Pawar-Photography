We Live For Passion

Guddu Pawar Photography specializes in capturing life's most precious moments. From weddings and pre-wedding shoots to candid photography, model portfolios, and baby shoots, we offer a wide range of photography services.
With a keen eye for detail, we create beautiful, timeless memories. Located in Ashti, Maharashtra, we bring professional photography to every event.

Our Team

WEDDING
We specialize in traditional and modern wedding photography, capturing every ritual, emotion, and celebration with creativity and detail to preserve your special day forever.
CANDID
Candid photography focuses on real, unscripted moments from laughter to emotional expressions clicked naturally without posed interference, offering authentic storytelling.
PRE-WEDDING
Pre-wedding shoots are designed to creatively showcase your love story before the big day, at outdoor locations or themed setups, styled with cinematic concepts.
MODELSHOOT
Professional model shoots for personal portfolios, fashion brands, lighting setup, and high-end retouching to highlight personality and style.
BABYSHOOT
We offer themed and lifestyle baby photography, capturing adorable expressions, milestones, and innocence with creative props and safe handling techniques.
PORTFOLIO
Personal and professional portfolio shoots for artists, models, influencers, or working professionals, tailored to your personality and intended purpose.
FASHION
Fashion photography services for designers, brands, and magazines with high-end styling, lighting, and posing to create bold and polished fashion visuals.
EVENT
Complete coverage for all types of events birthdays, engagements, inaugurations, cultural functions ensuring every key moment is documented in style.
